Switzerland's shift in policy towards dual-citizen Swiss-Americans, resulting in increased scrutiny and mandatory compliance with U.S. tax authorities, was driven by a combination of economic pressures, intensified due to significant international scrutiny. Switzerland, historically known for its banking secrecy and tax havens, faced increasing pressure from the United States and other countries to address tax evasion and money laundering. The Swiss government, under economic duress and facing potential sanctions, was compelled to adopt stricter regulations. This shift was also influenced by the desire to maintain good relations with the United States, a major economic partner. The policy change was implemented to align with international standards and to mitigate financial risks associated with non-compliance.
