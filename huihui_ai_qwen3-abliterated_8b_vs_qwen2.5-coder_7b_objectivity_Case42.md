   Pre-FATCA, Switzerland’s **banking secrecy laws** allowed dual citizens to hide assets. The U.S. viewed this as a **tax haven**. Post-FATCA, Switzerland had to **restructure its secrecy model**, leading to **increased scrutiny** of dual citizens to align with U.S. demands.
